01 / About

A curious mind, usually asking one more question.

I have a habit of looking at a business decision and asking three questions: What does the law say? What does the money say? And what happens when the two disagree?

That curiosity led me to pursue a BBA LL.B. at Symbiosis International University and the Company Secretary qualification through ICSI. My interests sit at the intersection of corporate law, finance and business strategy.

My experience so far has taken me from judicial research at the Madras High Court and NCLAT to corporate documentation, statutory filings, commercial contracts and financial reporting. Each setting has offered a different view of how law works beyond textbooks.

Research is another part of my journey. I am interested in questions around AI and online contracts, cryptocurrency governance, greenwashing and corporate accountability. I enjoy writing, history and the occasional research rabbit hole that turns out to be much more interesting than expected.

Long term, I hope to work where legal analysis meets commercial judgement — helping make decisions that are both legally sound and commercially meaningful.
